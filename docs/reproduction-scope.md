# Reproduction scope

## Included

- Local DiffNet prototype modules and tests.
- The opt-in DREAMPlace hook as a small patch.
- Small provenance manifests and selected GP/GGR logs.
- Audit documents that state exactly what was and was not executed.

## Excluded

- Complete DREAMPlace/Xplace repositories and submodules.
- ISPD2015 archive/raw designs and generated `ispd2015_fix` data.
- DEF/LEF/Verilog collateral, tensors, checkpoints, binaries, CUDA caches, core dumps.
- Innovus/PDK/commercial files.
- Paper PDFs and unrelated projects.

## Reproduction boundary

The evidence supports a partial local reproduction trail only. It does not support the claims `BASELINE_REPRODUCTION_VERIFIED` or `FULL_PAPER_REPRODUCTION_VERIFIED`.
